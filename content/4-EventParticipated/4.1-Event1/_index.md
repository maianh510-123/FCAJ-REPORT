# Summary Report: “Amazon Web Services [AWS]: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application featuring Cloud Kinetics & Renova Cloud”

### Purpose of the Event

- Provide a comprehensive overview of the Cloud Computing market and career opportunities for young talents in Vietnam.
- Orient mindset, adaptability skills, and effective ways to leverage AI in learning and professional work.
- Share practical experience on Data Engineering architectures, enterprise workflows, and career development paths from industry Cloud experts.

### List of Speakers

- **Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung** – Head of Solutions Architecture in Vietnam and Cambodia, Amazon Web Services
- **Mr. Khang Nguyen** – Solutions Architect, Cloud Kinetics
- **Ms. Nhu Tran** – Account Manager, Amazon Web Services
- **Mr. Vinh Banh** – Senior Data Engineer, Renova Cloud

### Key Highlights

#### Cloud Market & Recruitment Trends (Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung)

- **Rigorous market reality:** Enterprises prioritize a Cloud-first strategy and set high recruitment standards even for Intern / Junior positions (requiring knowledge of Kubernetes, Cloud Native AI, and practical project experience).
- **Workforce model in the AI era:** Personnel structures are shifting toward prioritizing Senior roles augmented by AI tools. Students need to proactively shorten their growth timeline to meet industry standards.
- **Formula for personal development:** Delivered value is built on three factors: Value = Capability x Visibility x Consistency. One must actively build visibility, maintain consistency, and continuously invest in oneself.

#### Data Engineering: Corporate Reality vs. Academia (Mr. Banh Cam Vinh)

- **Differences in data & requirements:** School assignments usually use clean data with flexible deadlines; enterprise reality involves messy data from multiple sources, constantly changing requirements, and direct impacts on business revenue/operations.
- **Architecture Thinking:** Tools and technologies evolve rapidly, but the core architectural framework (the DNA of a Data Platform: Ingestion, Processing, Storage, Governance, Analytics) remains constant.
- **AI Application & Communication:** Leverage AI as a tool to support architecture/code reviews; concurrently treat communication skills as a critical technical capability to clarify business problems.

#### Overcoming Fear & Seizing Opportunities (Ms. Nhu Tran)

- **Acknowledging and confronting fears:** Identify the nature of common fears (fear of failure, fear of falling short of expectations, fear of public speaking). Overcome them through open dialogue and continuous practice with an Internship Mindset.
- **Networking & Opportunity Seeking:** Engage in small talk to bridge the gap with managers and colleagues. Proactively seek opportunities in the "Blue Ocean"—less visible positions/projects—through persistence and building quality relationships.

#### Mindset & Skills for Young Engineers in the AI Era (Mr. Khang Nguyen)

- **Using AI purposefully:** "You can outsource your thinking, but you cannot outsource your understanding." AI serves as a catalyst (amplifier) to boost productivity, but learners must master fundamentals to validate AI output.
- **Importance of core fundamentals:** The university environment is where you pay to make mistakes and learn. Focus on mastering foundational subjects (Networking, Security, Systems) beyond mere exam expectations.
- **Candidate evaluation criteria:** Work is evaluated through 3 overlapping circles (Passion - Responsibility - Benefits). Recruiters prioritize candidates according to: Attitude > Aptitude > Experience.

### Key Takeaways

#### Design Thinking & Career Development

- **Business-first & Outcome-oriented:** Technology is merely a tool to solve business problems; always start with the actual needs of the customer/business.
- **Foundational Mindset:** Master the core mechanics of systems so as not to depend entirely on any specific tool or framework.
- **Attitude & Consistency:** Build resilience in the face of difficulties, view failure as a learning opportunity, and maintain a lifelong learning mindset.

#### Technical Architecture

- **Sustainable Architecture:** Design systems adhering to technical standards (such as the AWS Well-Architected Framework) to ensure Scalability, Security, and Reliability.
- **Balancing Speed & Quality:** Understand the trade-offs between deployment time, cost, and technical quality in Production environments.
- **Effective AI Utilization:** Treat AI as an assistant to boost productivity—using it for critique, verification, and product refinement rather than outsourcing core thinking.

#### Modernization Strategy & Personal Growth

- **Building a practical profile:** Write technical blogs and build side projects with near-enterprise-level completeness to pass automated resume screening rounds.
- **Expanding Networking:** Actively participate in tech communities (such as AWS First Cloud AI Journey) to learn from experienced predecessors and increase career opportunities through internal referrals.

### Application to Work

- **Enhancing coursework & side projects:** Incorporate real-world production criteria into personal projects, such as exception handling, data security, monitoring, and cost optimization.
- **Optimizing learning workflow with AI:** Apply AI to code review processes, explaining complex concepts, and suggesting architectural perspectives while cross-verifying with official documentation.
- **Honing soft skills:** Proactively ask questions during discussions, practice presentation skills, and strengthen connections with colleagues, mentors, and the broader community.

### Event Experience

The sharing session **“AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application featuring Cloud Kinetics & Renova Cloud”** offered valuable practical insights and multi-dimensional perspectives on the tech industry:

#### Learning from industry experts
- Speakers from AWS, Cloud Kinetics, and Renova Cloud shared candid insights into the recruitment landscape, helping clarify career development pathways.
- Received genuine advice from alumni on navigating pressure and making the most of university time.

#### Open interactive experience
- Energetic atmosphere with direct Q&A sessions, small talk practice, and addressing psychological fears when transitioning into a professional environment.
- Absorbed real-world lessons on data system design, project management mindsets, and effective cross-departmental collaboration (Business vs. Technical).

#### Summary takeaways
- Specialized technical skills are necessary, but a learning attitude, proactive networking, and problem-solving mindsets are the deciding factors for long-term career growth.
- AI is a powerful leverage tool when used correctly, but core thinking capability and deep understanding of business problems remain irreplaceable engineer values.
