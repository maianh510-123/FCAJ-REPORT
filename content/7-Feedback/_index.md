# FEEDBACK & SUGGESTIONS

### General Evaluation

**1. Work Environment:**
The working environment at First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) is highly professional, modern, and open. Proactiveness and creativity are always encouraged. Both the instructors and fellow interns in the program are always willing to share knowledge and support one another throughout the internship.

**2. Support from Mentors & Team Admin:**
The Mentors and Team Admin provided close guidance and dedicated support from the initial survey phase to actual system deployment. Instead of simply handing out solutions, Mentors guided our problem-solving mindset, encouraging us to explore official documentation and independently resolve technical challenges.

**3. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development:**
The 8-week internship brought a major breakthrough in both my hard and soft skills. I honed my experience with professional workflows (Agile/Scrum, Git Flow, GitHub Actions CI/CD), enhanced my UI/UX design mindset adhering to Accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1), and learned how to optimize client-side web application performance.

**4. Benefits & Support for Interns:**
The program provided optimal support in terms of flexible scheduling, equipment, and learning infrastructure (such as hands-on AWS credits/accounts). Specialized workshops and events featuring experts from AWS, Cloud Kinetics, and Renova Cloud offered invaluable real-world career orientation.
